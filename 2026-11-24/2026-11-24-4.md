## top-level paragraphs (field "paragraphs", shown when no level is chosen)
P1: Ryanair has quietly changed its seating policy for passengers who choose not to pay for a seat. Travellers who accept a free, randomly allocated seat will now only discover where they are sitting about three hours before departure.
P2: Previously, seat numbers were revealed during online check-in, which can open up to 24 hours before a flight. The change came into effect at the end of October, coinciding with the airline's switch to its winter schedule, but was introduced without any announcement.
P3: The move has frustrated some regular flyers. In the past, passengers who were given a middle seat could pay in the app to upgrade before check-in. That option has now disappeared, which could encourage more people to pay for seats in advance.
P4: Ryanair confirmed the change and defended it, saying the earlier system led to complaints over the summer from passengers who were unable to buy their preferred seat on the day of travel because it had already been allocated free of charge. Critics on social media, however, described it as another way of boosting profits.
