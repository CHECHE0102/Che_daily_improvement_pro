About

BinBin daily is a web app for learning English with real Irish news. Each day there are new articles at five levels (A2, B1, B2, C1, IELTS 7–8), a personal dictionary, quizzes, IELTS and TOEIC practice tests, and an adventure game across Ireland that you power up by studying.

BinBin daily is made and run by Ching Che Huang (CheChe), an independent developer based in Dublin, Ireland.

App: binbindaily.com · Contact: binbindaily.help@gmail.com

Privacy policy

Who we are

BinBin daily is made and run by Ching Che Huang (CheChe), an independent developer living in Ireland, who is responsible for your data (the "data controller" under the GDPR). For any privacy question, write to binbindaily.help@gmail.com or use Settings → Feedback; you'll get an answer within a month.

What we collect

  • Without signing in: your progress, words and game data stay on your phone (the browser's local storage) and are not uploaded.
  • When you sign in with Google: your Google account ID, display name and profile photo (your e-mail stays with Google's sign-in service), plus your words, reading and quiz history, game progress, outfits and news region, stored in the cloud so they follow you to a new phone.
  • Visible to other players: your nickname, flag, character look, level and power, leaderboard results (reading, IELTS, TOEIC, piano), likes received, and your nickname on the wishing-well winners list.
  • Plaza: your character's position on the game map (not your phone's GPS location) and what you say (chat bubbles) are shown live to players in the same area; bubbles disappear after a few seconds and are not stored. Players who visited the plaza recently appear there with their nickname and look, refreshed about every 15 minutes.
  • Party games, allies, gifts and invite codes: your nickname and game state in a room, ally links, gift records (both nicknames and the items), and who used whose invite code.
  • IG membership: the member code you request and your nickname; when you message BinBin on Instagram, your Instagram name appears in BinBin's Instagram inbox.
  • Fair-play checks: a daily automatic check that progress makes sense (for example coins or levels jumping suddenly); suspicious accounts are noted for the developer to review by hand.
  • If you turn on notifications: your push subscription, time zone, language and whether you've read today's news.
  • Usage statistics (only if you agree): a random device ID with the days this device opened the app, the first day, language, level, whether you're signed in, device type and how far you got in the welcome tour, and the day you first finished a story and a quiz. No name or contact details.
  • Error reports: when something breaks, the error message, app version, browser model and (if signed in) your account ID, to fix the problem.
  • Feedback: what you write, the optional contact you give, your nickname, account ID and device information, and our reply to you (kept with your account and deleted with it). When you report another player's name, that name and account ID are recorded too.

Why we may use it (legal basis)

  • To provide the service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): account, cloud sync, leaderboards, plaza, party games, gifts, invite codes, IG membership.
  • Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): usage statistics and push notifications. You can withdraw it any time in Settings; we then stop and delete the statistics already recorded.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): fair-play checks, error reports and handling your feedback, to keep the game fair and working and to answer you.

How we use it

Only to run and improve the app. We show no ads, never sell data and never share it with marketers. There are currently no paid purchases.

Who handles it

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud: sign-in, cloud database, live plaza and server code. The live plaza and server code run in the EU (Belgium); some other services may be outside the EU, and Google processes data under its data-protection terms, including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages (hosting and backup), jsDelivr (audio) and Google Fonts (fonts) see normal connection data such as your IP address.
  • Push services of Apple, Google and Mozilla deliver notifications to your device.
  • Google Sheets: a copy of feedback, so the developer can read it.
  • Google reCAPTCHA (App Check): checks in the background that requests really come from the BinBin site and not from an automated script, using technical information about your browser and device; Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI): only when you press "Write it for me" at the plaza CV desk, the education, work, skills and visa details you typed (never your name or contact details) go through our EU server to Anthropic, which writes the English CV and sends it back to your phone. We don't keep this text; Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its models and may keep it for a short time for safety.

How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Plaza chat bubbles: not stored; your plaza position is removed when you leave.
  • Usage statistics: until you withdraw consent, then deleted at once.
  • Error reports: deleted automatically after 90 days. Feedback: deleted automatically after 2 years.
  • Push details: deleted as soon as you turn notifications off.
  • CV desk: your answers and CV are kept only on your phone (cleared when you sign out); the server only counts how many AI writes you used today and this month.

Deleting your account

Settings → "Delete my account and data" has our server delete all your data: profile, words, every period's leaderboards, wishing well, likes, gifts, party rooms, fair-play records and your sign-in. Records another player keeps (such as a gift you sent them or a winners list) stay with them, but your nickname and account ID are removed. If any step fails, your account is not deleted and you can simply try again.

Your rights

Under the EU GDPR you can access, export, correct or delete your data and object to processing based on legitimate interests: Settings → Backup exports your learning records; for anything else, write to us. You can also complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission (www.dataprotection.ie).

BinBin daily is for people aged 13 and over; if you are under 13, please don't use it. If you are 13 to 15, please get a parent's or guardian's permission before signing in. Other players can see what you say in the plaza, so don't put your real name, school or contact details in your nickname or chat.

Cookies and local storage

We use no tracking cookies. The app uses your browser's local storage for your settings and progress, and keeps copies of the pages, stories and pictures you opened on your device so they work offline; the device ID for usage statistics is only created after you agree.

隱私權政策

我們是誰

BinBin daily 由住在愛爾蘭的個人開發者 Ching Che Huang（CheChe）製作與經營，也是你資料的負責人（GDPR 所說的「資料控制者」）。有任何隱私問題，請寄信到 binbindaily.help@gmail.com，或用「設定 → 意見回饋」告訴我，我會在一個月內回覆。

我們收集哪些資料

  • 不登入：學習紀錄、單字和遊戲進度只存在你的手機（瀏覽器的本機儲存空間），不會上傳。
  • 用 Google 登入：Google 帳號編號、顯示名稱、大頭貼（email 只由 Google 登入服務保管），以及你的單字、閱讀與測驗紀錄、遊戲進度、造型、新聞地區，存到雲端，讓你換手機也不會不見。
  • 其他玩家看得到的：你的暱稱、國旗、角色造型、等級與戰力、排行榜成績（閱讀、IELTS、多益、鋼琴）、收到的讚，以及許願池得獎名單上的暱稱。
  • 廣場：你的角色在遊戲地圖上的位置（不是手機的 GPS 定位）和你說的話（聊天泡泡），同一區的玩家即時看得到；聊天泡泡幾秒後就消失，不會保存。最近有來廣場的玩家，暱稱和造型會出現在廣場上，大約每 15 分鐘更新一次。
  • 多人遊戲、盟友、送禮、邀請碼：房間裡的暱稱和遊戲狀態、盟友關係、送禮紀錄（雙方暱稱與道具）、誰用了誰的邀請碼。
  • IG 會員：你申請時產生的會員碼和暱稱；你在 Instagram 私訊 BinBin 時，Instagram 帳號名稱會出現在 BinBin 的 Instagram 收件匣。
  • 公平遊戲檢查：每天自動比對進度是否合理（例如金幣、等級突然暴增），可疑的帳號會留下紀錄給開發者人工確認。
  • 開啟通知時：推播訂閱資訊、時區、語言，以及你當天是否讀過新聞。
  • 使用統計（只有你同意才會記錄）：一組隨機的裝置編號，記錄這台裝置哪幾天打開 app、第一次使用的日期、語言、程度、是否登入、裝置類型、新手引導走到第幾步、第一次讀完文章和做完測驗的日期。不含姓名或聯絡方式。
  • 錯誤回報：app 出錯時，錯誤訊息、版本、瀏覽器型號，以及（登入時）你的帳號編號，用來修正問題。
  • 意見回饋：你寫的內容、選填的聯絡方式、暱稱、帳號編號與裝置資訊，以及我們給你的回覆（存在你的帳號下，刪除帳號時一起刪除）。你檢舉別的玩家暱稱時，也會記下那個暱稱和帳號編號。

我們為什麼可以使用這些資料（法律依據）

  • 提供你要求的服務（GDPR 第 6 條第 1 項 b 款）：帳號、雲端同步、排行榜、廣場、多人遊戲、送禮、邀請碼、IG 會員。
  • 你的同意（第 6 條第 1 項 a 款）：使用統計、推播通知。你隨時可以在設定裡撤回，撤回後就不再記錄，已記錄的統計也會刪除。
  • 正當利益（第 6 條第 1 項 f 款）：公平遊戲檢查、錯誤回報、處理意見回饋，目的是讓遊戲公平、穩定，並回覆你的問題。

我們用資料做什麼

只用來提供 app 的功能和改進 app。不放廣告、不賣資料，也不把資料分享給行銷公司。目前沒有開放任何付費購買。

誰會經手這些資料

  • Google Firebase／Google Cloud：登入、雲端資料庫、即時廣場和伺服器程式。即時廣場和伺服器程式在歐盟（比利時）；其他部分服務可能在歐盟以外，Google 依其資料保護條款（包含歐盟標準契約條款）處理。
  • GitHub Pages、Cloudflare Pages（網站主機與備援）、jsDelivr（音檔）、Google Fonts（字型）：只會看到 IP 位址等一般連線資訊。

保存多久

  • 帳號資料：保存到你刪除帳號為止。
  • 廣場聊天泡泡：不保存；廣場位置在你離開後就刪除。
  • 使用統計：保存到你撤回同意為止，撤回時立即刪除。
  • 錯誤回報：90 天後自動刪除。意見回饋：2 年後自動刪除。
  • 推播資訊：關閉通知時立即刪除。
  • 履歷桌：你的回答和履歷只存在你的手機（登出時清除）；伺服器只記你今天和這個月用了幾次 AI。

刪除帳號

「設定 → 刪除我的帳號與資料」會由伺服器刪除你所有的資料：個人資料、單字、各期排行榜、許願池、按讚、送禮、多人房間、公平遊戲紀錄與 Google 登入帳號。別人保留的紀錄（例如你送給朋友的禮物、許願池得獎名單）會留給對方，但會拿掉你的暱稱和帳號編號。如果中途有任何一步沒有成功，帳號不會被刪掉，你可以再按一次。
