About

BinBin daily is a web app for learning English with real Irish news. Each day there are new articles at five levels (A2, B1, B2, C1, IELTS 7–8), a personal dictionary, quizzes, IELTS and TOEIC practice tests, and an adventure game across Ireland that you power up by studying.

BinBin daily is made and run by an independent developer based in Dublin, Ireland. Playing and learning are free. Some optional digital items can be bought in the Rainbow Vault.

Privacy policy

What we collect

  • Without signing in, your progress stays on your device (the browser's local storage).
  • When you sign in with Google: your Google account ID, display name, e-mail address and profile photo, plus your words, reading and quiz history, game progress and news region, are stored in Google Firebase.
  • Your nickname, flag and leaderboard results are visible to other signed-in players. In party games, your nickname and game state are visible to the other players in the room.
  • Purchases: we store which item you bought, its status (paid/refunded) and an order reference from Stripe. Card and billing details are collected and processed by Stripe/Link under their own privacy policies.
  • If you turn on notifications: your push subscription, time zone, language and whether you have read today's news.
  • Anonymous error reports to help fix bugs.

How we use it

Only to run the app: syncing progress, leaderboards, party games, reminders, delivering purchases, and fixing problems. No ads, no selling of data, no sharing with marketers.

Where it is stored

Google Firebase / Google Cloud (database, sign-in and the payment server), GitHub Pages (website hosting), Stripe (payments), and the push services of Apple, Google and Mozilla. Some servers may be outside the EU; these providers process data under their data-protection terms.

How long and your rights

Data is kept until you delete your account (Settings → "Delete my account and data"); purchase records may be kept as long as tax law requires. Under the GDPR you can access, export, correct or delete your data. Write to binbindaily.help@gmail.com, or complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission.

Cookies

No tracking cookies. The app uses your browser's local storage for settings and progress. Stripe's checkout page uses its own cookies for payment security.
