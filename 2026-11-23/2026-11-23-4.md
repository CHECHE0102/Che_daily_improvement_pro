P1: The regeneration of Dublin's O'Connell Street is likely to be dominated by one- and two-bedroom apartments, according to the senior civil servant chosen to lead the project.
P2: Robert Watt, a former secretary general of the Department of Health, was speaking at an architecture policy conference organised by the Office of Public Works.
