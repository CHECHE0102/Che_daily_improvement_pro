## top-level paragraphs (field "paragraphs", shown when no level is chosen)
P1: "We Are Born Good", a Hong Kong drama, claimed Best Narrative Feature at the 63rd Golden Horse Awards on Sunday, taking the most prestigious prize in Chinese-language cinema at a ceremony held at the Taipei Music Center.
P2: Having entered the night with nine nominations, the film left with two trophies, also winning Best Original Screenplay, which director Yeung Leung-chuen shared with his co-writer. In the top category it beat a strong field that included "Girl", "Double Happiness", "Arrested Memory" and "We Are All Strangers".
P3: Set in Hong Kong, the film follows a former prisoner trying to rebuild his life by running a cafe. After a young aspiring actress receives a botched food order, a minor dispute between the two gradually escalates into a bitter feud.
P4: Accepting the award, Yeung said the trophy felt heavy but that he was able to lift it because everyone involved had been wonderful. He also urged the public to support cinemas, reminding them that films cannot survive without audiences.
P5: Elsewhere, "Double Happiness" and "Kung Fu" each collected three awards, with Joseph Hsu named Best Director and Liu Kuan-ting Best Actor. Hong Kong's Angela Yuen won Best Actress, while Shu Qi took Best New Director for "Girl", a coming-of-age story set in Keelung in 1988.
