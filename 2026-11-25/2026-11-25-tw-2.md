## top-level paragraphs (field "paragraphs", shown when no level is chosen)
P1: Academia Sinica, Taiwan's foremost research institution, has renewed its scientific partnership with France's National Centre for Scientific Research (CNRS), extending a relationship that began in 2018 until at least 2030. The agreement, first renewed in 2022, also raises the possibility of an international research laboratory being set up at Academia Sinica.
P2: Academia Sinica president Chen Chien-jen signed the memorandum of understanding with French representatives. He and CNRS chairman and chief executive Thierry Dauxois had laid the groundwork for the deal when they met at this year's Science and Technology in Society forum in Kyoto.
P3: The two bodies already cooperate through researcher exchanges, joint projects and educational programs designed to train specialists. Their shared work ranges from ion channels in nerve cells and topological materials to the analysis of protein structures, as well as collaboration in the humanities.
P4: The new memorandum focuses on regularly scheduled academic seminars, research grants and mechanisms that support joint and interdisciplinary research. Ocean sciences, biobanks, quantum physics and biofuels were named as future areas of cooperation, fields described as vital for tackling global scientific and social challenges.
P5: According to CNRS, international research laboratories are a key tool for global collaboration because they pool researchers, expertise and resources from partner institutions. A lab at Academia Sinica would give the two sides a long-term platform for bilateral cooperation.
