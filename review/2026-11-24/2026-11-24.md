Q1 [main]: Which statement best captures the main point of the passage?
   A. Anne O'Connor resigned from the HSE to take up a post in another organisation.
   B. The Minister for Health removed the HSE chief executive against the wishes of the board.
   C. The Taoiseach will address the Dáil after the HSE board abruptly removed its chief executive, a move that has led to demands for accountability.  <- answer
   D. The Cabinet has cut the HSE's budget by €780 million because of overspending.
   explanation: The passage opens by saying the Taoiseach will "address the Dáil today regarding the abrupt removal of Anne O'Connor", which "has prompted considerable demands for accountability". She did not resign (A), the decision "fell exclusively within the remit of the board" (B), and the €780 million was a "supplementary allocation", not a cut (D).
Q2 [infer]: What can be inferred about the Minister for Health?

   B. She publicly opposed the board's decision.

   D. She intends to replace the HSE board.
   explanation: The minister "conceded that she was informed in September of a procedure that could potentially result in Ms O'Connor's removal", so she knew in advance. She "expressed her support for it" (not B); nothing suggests C or D.

   A. To argue that Ms O'Connor's removal was unfair.

   D. To give background showing that Ms O'Connor's time in charge had been troubled.  <- answer
   explanation: These details follow the statement that "Ms O'Connor's tenure was characterised by considerable controversy": a warning of a "deficit of 1,000 staff" and extra money "owing to recurrent overspending".
