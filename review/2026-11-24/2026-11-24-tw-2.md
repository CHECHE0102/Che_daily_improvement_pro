## level B1
B1 P1 S1: The Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Taiwan said on Tuesday that the United States had recently confirmed that its policy on Taiwan has not changed.
    zh: 台灣外交部星期二表示，美國最近確認其對台政策沒有改變。
B1 P1 S2: The comment came after Chinese state media reported a surprising remark by US President Donald Trump.
    zh: 這番說法是在中國官方媒體報導美國總統川普一句令人意外的話之後提出的。
B1 P2 S1: On November 19, the Chinese news agency Xinhua reported on a meeting between Trump and Chinese leader Xi Jinping in Shenzhen, held during the APEC summit.
    zh: 11月19日，中國新華社報導了川普與中國領導人習近平在亞太經合會峰會期間於深圳舉行的會面。
B1 P2 S2: According to the report, Xi told Trump to handle the Taiwan issue carefully, and Trump said the US does not support Taiwan independence.
    zh: 根據報導，習近平要川普謹慎處理台灣問題，而川普說美國不支持台灣獨立。
B1 P3 S1: The White House has not confirmed or denied this.
    zh: 白宮沒有證實也沒有否認這件事。
B1 P3 S2: Its own summary of the talks did not mention Taiwan at all, which was also true after the previous meetings between the two leaders.
    zh: 它自己的會談摘要完全沒有提到台灣，兩位領導人之前的幾次會面後也是如此。
B1 P4 S1: Hans Chiao, a senior official in the North American affairs department of the ministry, told reporters that Washington had repeated its position to Taipei.
    zh: 外交部北美事務部門的高階官員焦國祐告訴記者，華府已向台北重申其立場。
B1 P4 S2: However, he did not say whether Trump had actually made the remark.
    zh: 不過，他沒有說川普是否真的說過那句話。
B1 P4 S3: He described Chinese state media as propaganda tools for Beijing and said Secretary of State Marco Rubio had confirmed the policy many times.
    zh: 他形容中國官方媒體是北京的宣傳工具，並說國務卿盧比歐已多次確認這項政策。
B1 P5 S1: US policy is based on laws and agreements such as the Taiwan Relations Act and the Six Assurances.
    zh: 美國的政策是以《台灣關係法》和「六項保證」等法律與協議為基礎。
B1 P5 S2: The US says it opposes any change to the current situation by either side and wants differences to be solved peacefully.
    zh: 美國表示，它反對任何一方改變現狀，並希望以和平方式解決分歧。

## level B2
B2 P1 S1: The Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) in Taiwan said on Tuesday that Washington had recently reaffirmed that its Taiwan policy remains unchanged, days after Chinese state media claimed that US President Donald Trump had told Xi Jinping he does not support Taiwan independence.
    zh: 台灣外交部星期二表示，華府最近重申其對台政策維持不變；就在幾天前，中國官方媒體聲稱美國總統川普曾告訴習近平，他不支持台灣獨立。
B2 P2 S1: The claim appeared in a Xinhua report on November 19 about the meeting of the two leaders in Shenzhen on the sidelines of the APEC summit.
    zh: 這個說法出現在新華社11月19日的報導中，內容是關於兩位領導人在亞太經合會峰會場邊於深圳的會面。
B2 P2 S2: According to Xinhua, Xi urged Trump to handle Taiwan-related issues appropriately in order to avoid major setbacks in relations between the two powers.
    zh: 據新華社報導，習近平敦促川普妥善處理涉台問題，以避免兩大強權關係出現重大倒退。
B2 P3 S1: The White House has neither confirmed nor denied the account.
    zh: 白宮對這個說法既未證實也未否認。
B2 P3 S2: Its official readout of the talks did not mention Taiwan, and it issued only a general summary of the Asia trip by Trump, as it had done after the two previous meetings of the leaders.
    zh: 它對會談的官方紀要沒有提到台灣，只發布了一份川普亞洲行的概括摘要，就像兩位領導人前兩次會面後的做法一樣。
B2 P4 S1: Speaking to reporters, Hans Chiao, deputy director-general of the Department of North American Affairs at MOFA, said the US had restated its position to Taipei, though he declined to say whether Trump had made the remark.
    zh: 外交部北美司副司長焦國祐對記者表示，美國已向台北重申其立場，但他不願透露川普是否說過那句話。
B2 P4 S2: He dismissed Chinese state media as propaganda outlets and stressed that Taiwan is a sovereign, democratic country.
    zh: 他駁斥中國官方媒體是宣傳機器，並強調台灣是一個主權獨立的民主國家。
B2 P4 S3: Chiao added that Secretary of State Marco Rubio and other senior officials had repeatedly reaffirmed the policy.
    zh: 焦國祐補充說，國務卿盧比歐和其他高層官員已多次重申這項政策。
B2 P5 S1: According to the American Institute in Taiwan, US policy is guided by the Taiwan Relations Act, three joint communiqués with China and the Six Assurances.
    zh: 根據美國在台協會的說法，美國政策以《台灣關係法》、與中國的三份聯合公報以及「六項保證」為指導。
B2 P5 S2: Washington opposes unilateral changes to the status quo and expects cross-strait differences to be resolved peacefully.
    zh: 華府反對單方面改變現狀，並期望兩岸分歧以和平方式解決。

    zh: 這項保證是在中國官方媒體一篇報導之後提出的，該報導指稱川普總統說過美國不支持台灣獨立。

    zh: 新華社11月19日發布的這篇報導，描述了川普與習近平在亞太經合會峰會場邊於深圳舉行的會面。

    zh: 他將中國官方媒體形容為北京的宣傳工具，堅稱台灣是一個主權獨立的民主國家，並指出國務卿盧比歐已多次重申這項政策。

    zh: 在以《台灣關係法》、三份聯合公報和「六項保證」為基礎的長期架構下，美國反對單方面改變現狀，並期望兩岸爭端能和平解決。
