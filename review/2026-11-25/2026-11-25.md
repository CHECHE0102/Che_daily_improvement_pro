Q1 [main]: Which statement best captures the main point of the passage?
   A. Ireland has finished restoring its wetlands and peatlands ahead of an EU deadline.
   B. The European Commission has rejected Ireland's nature restoration plan for lacking targets.
   C. Ireland has published a legally required draft plan with concrete restoration targets, though critics say its success depends on long-term funding.  <- answer
   D. Ireland has decided to plant three billion trees by 2030 to meet its climate goals.
   explanation: The passage describes a draft plan that is "a statutory obligation rather than a discretionary policy", lists "quantifiable targets", and ends with groups warning that success is "contingent upon multi-annual funding". Restoration is not finished (A), the Commission has not rejected it (B), and three billion trees is the EU-wide figure, with Ireland's share about 30 million (D).
Q2 [infer]: What can be inferred from the comparison with County Offaly?
   A. The upper estimate for the restoration area is large enough to be significant on a national scale.  <- answer
   B. All of the restoration work will take place in County Offaly.
   C. County Offaly has the most degraded land in Ireland.
   D. The lower estimate of 476 square kilometres is larger than County Offaly.
   explanation: The area "ranges from 476 to 2,039 square kilometres, the latter exceeding the total area of County Offaly", so the comparison shows how sizeable the upper figure is. Only "the latter" (2,039) exceeds Offaly, ruling out (D), and nothing says the work is located in Offaly (B) or that its land is the most degraded (C).
Q3 [purpose]: Why does the writer mention BirdWatch Ireland and the Climate Change Advisory Council in the final paragraph?
   A. To show that environmental groups oppose the EU regulation.
   B. To explain who designed the pilot projects.
   C. To praise the €20 million pathfinder fund as sufficient.
   D. To introduce a reservation about whether the plan can succeed without secure long-term funding.  <- answer
   explanation: The paragraph opens with "Nevertheless" and reports that these organisations "maintain that the success of the plan is contingent upon multi-annual funding", adding a note of caution after the minister's positive view. They do not oppose the regulation (A), design the pilots (B) or call the fund sufficient (C).
